Where this comes from

  • Every Uber and Lyft trip in New York City is published by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission. This uses 51.5 million non-shared rides from July to September 2026; the trend charts use matched rides from March 2025 to September 2026.
  • Prices compare only quick-pickup rides on the same route and hour, so cheaper wait-longer ride options don't skew the result. Promo codes and surge on a given day aren't visible, so treat this as "what usually happens," not a live quote.
  • Some of Uber's long waits are discounted rides that riders chose, so Uber's wait numbers are an upper bound. Riders who gave up aren't recorded at all.
  • A route needs 30+ rides on each app. Thinner routes fall back to a borough-level comparison, and the card says so.
