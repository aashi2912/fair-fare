| 5 | Same month, year over year for waits | Rules out seasonality | Month-to-month resolution |
| 6 | Cut four early claims | They failed the matching or seasonality checks | Two of the most shareable charts |

| 8b | Built on Uber Assistant | The channel and the ledger already exist; the gap is one question at one moment | A standalone product |
